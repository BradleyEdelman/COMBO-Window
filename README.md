# COMBO Window

This repository contains **3D printer (and laser cutter) files**, as well as **analysis and plotting code** associated with the validation of the **COMBO Window**—a chronic cranial implant for multiscale circuit interrogation in mice.

## 📄 Related Publication

### **The COMBO Window: A Chronic Cranial Implant for Multiscale Circuit Interrogation in Mice**  
**B. J. Edelman & D. Siegenthaler et al., PLOS Biology, 2024**  
- Introduces **a novel cranial implant** that enables **long-term, multimodal neural recordings** in head-fixed moving mice.  
- **Key contributions:**
  - **Supports multiple imaging modalities** (two-photonimaging, functional ultrasound imaging, electrophysiology).  
  - Enables **stable, chronic recordings months after implantation** while maintaining high-quality optical access.  
  - **Designed for easy fabrication and open-source sharing** using 3D printing and laser cutting.  

📄 [Read the full paper](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.3002664)

## Summary  
The **COMBO Window** is an **open-source cranial implant** designed for long-term **multimodal neural recordings in mice**. This repository provides:
- **3D printer & laser cutter files** for fabrication.  
- **Analysis & plotting scripts** for validation data.  
- **Open-access datasets & code** for reproducibility and further development.
